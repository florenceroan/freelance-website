About

Bridging research, education, and practice

In graduate school, the questions that drew me to research were mechanistic ones: how systems work, how pathways connect. Clinical training expanded those questions. I found myself asking not just what the evidence shows, but what it means for the person in the room. Both frames inform how I approach medical and scientific communication. This is also what drew me to continuing medical education, where effective communication must do more than inform — it must engage, persuade, and drive meaningful change.

Language matters, not just for clarity, but also for who gets to be included in the conversation. I bring that same care to how I work with clients. I work collaboratively and with genuine investment, helping you close the gap between evidence and patient care.

Precise, evidence-based writing that serves the science and the people who depend on it.
Services

How I can help

I work with clients who need complex science communicated clearly for education, publications, or presentations.

CME Content Development

I create CME content grounded in instructional design and adult learning principles, focused on measurable outcomes. Whether developing a needs assessment or a slide deck, I apply an analytical lens to ensure each deliverable advances a defined educational outcome.

  • Needs assessments, gap analyses, and root cause analyses
  • Educational activities and patient cases
  • Slide decks
  • White papers
  • Outcomes reports

Writing & Research Support

From manuscript preparation to early-stage study design, I support clinicians and researchers who need a collaborator with both scientific depth and practical experience.

  • Manuscript writing and submission
  • Study design guidance for retrospective, observational, and QI/QA projects
  • IRB and IACUC protocol preparation and submission support
  • Data management, preparation, and basic analysis
  • Data visualization for manuscripts and presentations
